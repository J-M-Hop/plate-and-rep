Plate & Rep Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Plate & Rep ("the app") is a workout tracker built by JMHop. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

The short version

Plate & Rep does not collect, transmit, or share any of your data. Everything you log stays on your device.

What the app stores

The app stores the information you enter: workouts, exercises, sets, reps, weights, plans, personal bests, and your settings. This data is saved only in the app's private storage on your device.

What the app does not do

  • It does not require an account or sign-in.
  • It does not send your data to any server, to the developer, or to any third party.
  • It does not use analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting services.
  • It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or other personal information.

Exporting and importing

You can export your workout data to a file and import it later. Exports are created only when you choose to export, and you decide where the file goes (for example, saving it or sharing it through another app). The developer never receives your exports.

Links to other sites

The app contains one link to the developer's Twitch channel. If you tap it, it opens in your browser or the Twitch app, and Twitch's own privacy policy applies from that point.

Your control over your data

Because your data lives only on your device, you control it. Uninstalling the app deletes all data stored in it. Exported files are yours to keep or delete.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new date.
